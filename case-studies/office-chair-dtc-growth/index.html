Office Furniture — Paid Advertising & Marketplace

Scaling Spend Without Losing the Margin

An office chair brand needed paid acquisition and marketplace strategy that scaled without eroding margin — in a category where shipping and returns costs bite hard on every bulky, high-AOV order.

Book a Growth Call →▷ See More Case Studies
The Challenge

Volume Isn't the Goal When Every Order Is Bulky

High average order values and bulky-item logistics meant this brand couldn't just chase traffic volume. Every acquisition channel had to be evaluated on real unit economics — landed cost, return rate, contribution margin — not surface-level metrics like clicks or impressions.

The brand needed a partner who would optimize for profitable growth even when it meant pulling back on channels that looked good on a top-line dashboard.

At A Glance
  • Category: Office chairs
  • Channels: Paid advertising & marketplace
  • Engagement: Margin-aware acquisition strategy
  • Result: 25%+ average month-over-month growth
Our Approach

Growth Built Around Unit Economics

  • Rebuilt paid advertising strategy around margin-aware acquisition targets instead of raw traffic or CPC goals
  • Marketplace listing and catalog optimization suited to a considered, high-AOV purchase
  • Ongoing campaign management across paid and marketplace channels, reallocating spend toward what actually held margin
  • Reporting tied to contribution margin and profitability — not vanity metrics like impressions or raw click volume
The Results

Growth That Held Up Under Scrutiny

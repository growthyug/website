Why CM3, Not Just ROAS

A campaign can show a strong 3.5x ROAS in Ads Manager and still lose money once product cost, shipping, gateway fees, returns and marketing spend are all accounted for. Contribution Margin strips that illusion out, layer by layer.

CM1 — Gross Margin

CM1 = Revenue − Product COGS

CM2 — After Fulfillment

CM2 = CM1 − Shipping − Payment Gateway Fees − Returns/RTO Cost

CM3 — Real Per-Order Profit

CM3 = CM2 − Marketing Cost per Order

CM3 is the number that should decide whether to scale a channel, not platform ROAS alone. For a full walkthrough of blended CAC and how it feeds the marketing cost line here, see our Ecommerce CAC Playbook or model acquisition cost first with the CAC & LTV:CAC Calculator.

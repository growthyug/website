Amazon Advertising Playbook

The Amazon PPC & ACoS
Optimization Playbook

How to structure high-performing Amazon ad campaigns, eliminate wasted search term spend, and scale keyword ranking without sacrificing margins.

Most Amazon sellers face a common dilemma: when they cut advertising spend to lower ACoS, organic sales and keyword ranking plummet. When they increase bids to drive rank, profit margins vanish.

1. The Four-Tier Amazon Campaign Architecture

High-growth brands do not dump all keywords into a single campaign. They segment campaigns by keyword intent and match types to maintain granular bid control.

Tier 1: Auto Discovery Campaigns

Run low-bid Auto campaigns with Close Match and Loose Match targeting solely to discover newly trending search queries and competitor ASINs.

Tier 2: Manual Exact Ranking Campaigns

Isolate your top 10–20 root keywords into dedicated single-keyword or tightly clustered Exact Match campaigns with aggressive Top-of-Search (First Page) bid modifiers.

Tier 3: Product Attribute Targeting (PAT) & ASIN Defense

Target competitor product detail pages where your product has a clear competitive advantage (better price, higher ratings, or faster Prime delivery), while actively bidding on your own ASINs to defend cross-sells.

Tier 4: Branded Search Defense

Run dedicated Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Products on your exact brand terms with low bids to prevent competitors from stealing high-intent buyers searching directly for your name.

2. Search Term Isolation & Negative Keyword Harvesting

Wasted spend occurs when the same search query triggers ads in multiple campaigns with differing bids. Implementing strict negative exact match cross-negation prevents your Auto and Broad campaigns from bidding against your Exact ranking campaigns.

3. The TACoS Target Rule

Evaluate your Total Advertising Cost of Sales (TACoS) weekly. As ad-driven sales build review velocity and sales momentum, organic ranking rises. A healthy brand typically targets a TACoS between 8% and 15%, ensuring strong total net contribution margin.

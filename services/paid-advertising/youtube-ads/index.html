High-Intent Video

Scale Past Meta Ad Saturation With YouTube Video Action

When brands hit diminishing returns on Meta Ads, YouTube offers unprecedented reach combined with Google's intent-driven search audience signals. By targeting users who recently searched for your competitors or category keywords on Google Search and Maps, YouTube ads reach buyers at peak consideration.

We build high-converting direct-response video funnels combining 15-second skippable in-stream hooks, high-converting product overlays, and vertical YouTube Shorts ads that convert viewers directly into paying customers.

From scriptwriting and hook architecture to audience segmenting and tCPA bidding, we manage your entire YouTube acquisition engine.

Our Video Strategy
  • Custom Intent Targeting: Bidding on users searching for competitor brand keywords and commercial buying terms on Google.
  • Hook Rate Optimization: Crafting the first 5 seconds to eliminate unqualified clicks while hooking high-LTV buyers.
  • YouTube Shorts Ad Scaling: Direct-response vertical video feeds optimized for mobile impulse purchasing.
  • Demand Gen Multi-Format: Synchronizing YouTube videos, Discover feeds, and Gmail ad placements.

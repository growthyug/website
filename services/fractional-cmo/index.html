The Challenge

Why Scaling D2C Brands Hit The ₹1Cr/mo Ceiling

When brands grow past early product-market fit, founders get trapped managing tactical channel managers and isolated freelancers. Nobody is steering the high-level P&L, contribution margin, or cross-channel attribution.

Hiring a full-time Chief Marketing Officer costs ₹40L–₹60L/year plus equity, often before your revenue justifies a permanent C-suite salary. Junior media buyers, on the other hand, lack the holistic strategic perspective needed to scale across Amazon, Meta, and Shopify simultaneously.

A Fractional CMO bridges this exact gap: veteran growth direction, revenue roadmap execution, and vendor governance on a flexible, outcome-driven retainer.

Core Pillars Of Leadership
  • Growth Architecture: Full-funnel modeling across Paid Ads, Marketplaces, Organic Search, and Retention.
  • Unit Economics Governance: Modeling MER, Contribution Margin, and Break-Even ROAS to protect cash flow.
  • Creative Velocity: Establishing standardized creative testing pipelines and UGC briefs that scale Meta ad spend.
  • Vendor & Team Leadership: Holding in-house teams, freelancers, and media buyers strictly accountable to KPIs.
  • Omnichannel Expansion: Orchestrating Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, and Quick Commerce launch playbooks.

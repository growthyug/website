## 2. Key Content & Technical Gaps Identified

### Gap 1: Interactive Tool Utility (High Inbound Link Potential)
- **Observation:** Most competitors only offer static PDFs behind gated email forms.
- **GrowthYug Opportunity:** Published the **Interactive Ecommerce ROAS & ACoS Calculator** (`/resources/ecommerce-roas-acos-calculator/`) with real-time DOM calculation, breakeven analysis, and zero gated friction.

### Gap 2: Indian Marketplace Advertising Depth (Amazon + Flipkart + Meesho)
- **Observation:** US agencies write exclusively about Amazon.com / Walmart. Indian agencies write generic social media content.
- **GrowthYug Opportunity:** Dedicated service hubs for Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho combined with our in-depth Amazon PPC & TACoS guides.

### Gap 3: Complete D2C Financial Frameworks (Blended CAC vs Contribution Margin)
- **Observation:** 90% of agency blogs discuss "ROAS" without explaining Contribution Margin 3 (CM3), CAC Payback, and Net Profitability.
- **GrowthYug Opportunity:** GrowthYug's published insights provide exact mathematical formulas, cohort retention schedules, and unit economic models.

### Gap 4: Modern Technical SEO & AEO / GEO Readiness
- **Observation:** Competitors have legacy bloated WordPress sites with slow LCP (>3.8s) and broken or missing Schema.org markup.
- **GrowthYug Opportunity:** 
  - Ultra-fast native static architecture (<1.0s LCP).
  - Rich JSON-LD on every page (`Service`, `Article`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization`, `Person`).
  - Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) direct-definition formatting for AI search engines.

### Why Brands Choose GrowthYug
Scaling an eCommerce brand in today's competitive landscape requires more than just launching ads or running basic SEO. Rising customer acquisition costs (CAC), signal loss across ad networks, fierce marketplace competition, and complex omnichannel attribution mean that brands cannot afford disjointed marketing execution. 

GrowthYug solves this by deploying a unified eCommerce Revenue Architecture covering:

#### 1. Full-Funnel Paid Advertising (Meta, Google, YouTube)
We architect full-funnel paid media campaigns focused on profitable new customer acquisition (nCAC) and blended Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER). From high-converting UGC creative frameworks to advanced audience segmentation, Google Performance Max feed optimization, and structured bid scaling, our media buying is scientific and margin-first.

#### 2. Marketplace Management & Advertising (Amazon & Flipkart)
We engineer marketplace growth across Amazon (India, US, UK, UAE) and Flipkart. Our marketplace specialists handle catalog management, listing SEO, high-impact A+ Content / Brand Stores, inventory planning, and granular Sponsored Products/Brands/Display PPC campaigns designed to lower Total Advertising Cost of Sales (TACoS) while growing organic ranking.

#### 3. Shopify Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
Traffic is useless if your store doesn't convert. We audit every step of your buyer journey — homepage hero architecture, mobile navigation, collection page filters, product page trust badges, sticky add-to-cart elements, cart drawer cross-sells, and checkout friction points — lifting conversion rates and Average Order Value (AOV).

#### 4. Ecommerce SEO & Generative AI Search (AEO)
We execute deep technical SEO audits, category page keyword architectures, structured schema data implementation, and search-intent content hubs that capture high-intent commercial buyers on Google, Bing, and emerging AI answer engines.

#### 5. Fractional CMO & Growth Advisory
For scaling brands that need executive growth direction without the overhead of a full-time executive, our Fractional CMO service provides financial modeling, channel mix strategy, agency coordination, and roadmap execution.

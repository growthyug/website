## 2. Lead Qualification & Scoring Matrix

To protect agency margins and focus resources on accounts capable of recurring retainers, every lead is scored out of **100 points**:

| Criterion | High Value (Max Points) | Medium Value | Disqualifier / Low Value (0 Points) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Revenue (GMV)** | > $30k/mo / > ₹15L/mo (25 pts) | $10k–$30k/mo / ₹5L–₹15L/mo (15 pts) | Pre-revenue / < $3k/mo (0 pts) |
| **Current Monthly Ad Spend** | > $5,000/mo / > ₹1.5L/mo (20 pts) | $1,500–$5,000/mo / ₹50k–₹1.5L/mo (10 pts) | Zero ad budget / Expects free trial (0 pts) |
| **Gross Margin Profile** | > 60% Gross Margin (15 pts) | 40% – 60% Margin (10 pts) | < 30% Margin (0 pts) |
| **Store & Product Validation** | High quality, 50+ reviews, strong product-market fit (15 pts) | Moderate validation, good website (10 pts) | Broken website, drop-shipped low quality (0 pts) |
| **Decision-Maker Access** | Direct conversation with Founder / CMO / CEO (15 pts) | Middle manager / Project coordinator (8 pts) | Anonymous buyer, zero communication (0 pts) |
| **Market & Geography** | US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Established India D2C (10 pts) | Developing regions (5 pts) | Restricted or unsupportable markets (0 pts) |

### **Lead Tiering Action Plan:**
- **Tier A (Score 80–100):** Top Priority. Respond within 15 minutes. Offer custom 10-minute video audit + immediate discovery call.
- **Tier B (Score 55–79):** High Opportunity. Standard diagnostic proposal + invite to discovery call.
- **Tier C (Score 40–54):** Fixed-Price Productized Audit only ($499 / ₹25,000) before offering monthly retainer.
- **Reject (Score < 40):** Decline politely or refer out.

## 3. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) by Stage

### **Stage 1: Lead Intake & Initial Response**
- **SOP Target Time:** Within 30 minutes of RFP publication or inbound message.
- **Action:**
  1. Inspect client website, store niche, and existing ad library / marketplace listings.
  2. Fill out Qualification Scorecard.
  3. Customize the corresponding diagnostic proposal template (Meta, Google, Amazon, CRO, etc.).

### **Stage 2: Discovery Call Framework (20 Minutes)**
- **Minutes 0–5 (Rapport & Context):** "Tell me about how you started {{Brand}}, what your current growth goals are for this quarter, and what's currently holding you back."
- **Minutes 5–12 (Diagnostic Deep-Dive):**
  - "What is your current Blended CAC and target ROAS/MER?"
  - "What is your best-selling SKU and what is its gross margin?"
  - "What have you tested previously and why did it fail?"
- **Minutes 12–17 (Prescription & Case Framework):** Walk through GrowthYug's specific 3-phase execution framework for their specific bottleneck.
- **Minutes 17–20 (Closing Next Step):** "Based on our discussion, we will prepare a 1-page Scope of Work outlining deliverables and milestones. Let’s reconvene on Thursday to finalize."

### **Stage 3: Proposal to Contract Closing**
- Deliver clean, milestone-based Scope of Work within 24 hours of discovery call.
- Secure contract signature and initial retainer invoice / platform milestone escrow funding prior to kickoff.

## 6. KPI Tracking & Performance Metrics

- **Top of Funnel:**
  - Qualified Opportunities Identified: Target **15–20 / week**
  - Proposals Submitted: Target **10–12 / week**
- **Middle of Funnel:**
  - Proposal-to-Response Rate: Target **> 25%**
  - Discovery Calls Booked: Target **3–5 / week**
- **Bottom of Funnel:**
  - Call-to-Close Rate: Target **> 30%**
  - New Retainers Signed: Target **2–4 / month**
  - Average Retainer Value: Target **$1,500 – $4,500/mo** (or ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000/mo)
  - Blended Platform ROI: Target **> 8x** (Revenue vs. platform/connect fees)

# GrowthYug — 30-Minute Ecommerce Growth Audit & Pitch Playbook

**Target Audience:** D2C Founders, Ecommerce Directors, Marketplace Heads generating ₹5L/mo to ₹1Cr/mo.  
**Objective:** Convert discovery calls into high-ticket growth retainers (₹75,000 to ₹2,50,000/month).

---

## 🎯 THE 5-PHASE AUDIT CALL STRUCTURE (30 MINUTES)

```
[00-05 min] → Diagnostic Rapport & Revenue Constraints
[05-15 min] → Live Funnel & Unit Economics Breakdown (The Leak Audit)
[15-22 min] → The 90-Day Full-Funnel Roadmap (The Solution)
[22-27 min] → Commercial Retainer & ROI Math (The Offer)
[27-30 min] → Next Steps & Technical Access Handover
```

---

## 🔍 PHASE 1: DIAGNOSTIC QUESTIONS (00–05 MIN)

Ask these precise questions to identify their primary constraint before showing any slides:

1. **Revenue Baseline:** *"What was your net delivered GMV last month across Shopify vs. Amazon vs. Flipkart?"*
2. **Current Margin Health:** *"What is your current product COGS and average return/RTO rate?"*
3. **Channel Performance:** *"What are you spending monthly on Meta and Google Ads, and what blended ROAS are you seeing in ad accounts?"*
4. **The Bottleneck:** *"If you doubled your ad spend tomorrow, what breaks first — ad fatigue, cash flow, stock inventory, or fulfillment?"*

---

## 🔬 PHASE 2: LIVE AUDIT FRAMEWORK (05–15 MIN)

Open their live store and marketplace listings on screen sharing. Walk through the 3 core leakages:

### 1. Acquisition & Ad Account Leakage
- **Meta Ads:** Are they testing creatives horizontally in a DCT sandbox, or wasting budget tweaking interest audiences?
- **Amazon PPC:** Are their Auto and Broad campaigns cannibalizing their Manual Exact ranking keywords? What is their current TACoS?
- **Google Search / PMax:** Are branded search terms inflating their reported PMax ROAS while non-brand terms lose money?

### 2. Product Detail Page & Conversion Rate Leakage
- **Mobile Viewport:** Is there a sticky Add-to-Cart bar on mobile?
- **Trust Elements:** Are delivery timelines, pincode checkers, and COD badges immediately visible above the fold?
- **Checkout Flow:** Are they using 1-click headless checkout (GoKwik, Fastrr, Shop Pay) or losing 60%+ of users on multi-step forms?

### 3. Unit Economics & Break-Even Modeling
- Open `https://www.growthyug.in/resources/ecommerce-roas-acos-calculator/` live on screen.
- Plug in their actual AOV, COGS%, and RTO%.
- Show them their **True Break-Even ROAS** and **Maximum allowable ACoS**.
- *Insight statement:* *"Right now, your reported ROAS is 2.8x, but with a 15% RTO and 35% COGS, your real contribution margin is only +4%. We need to lift conversion by 25% to take your net margin to +18%."*

---

## 🚀 PHASE 3: THE 90-DAY FULL-FUNNEL ROADMAP (15–22 MIN)

Present the GrowthYug 3-Phase Execution Sprint:

### Month 1: Foundation & Friction Elimination
- Audit and restructure Meta Ads Manager (2-campaign CBO/ASC framework).
- Amazon search term negative match isolation and Top-of-Search bid adjustments.
- Deploy 12 quick-win Shopify CRO fixes (Sticky ATC, Pincode checker, Cart drawer cross-sells).
- Set up real-time MER and CAC tracking dashboard.

### Month 2: Creative & Channel Velocity
- Launch weekly 3-second hook creative testing pipeline (3 hooks &times; 2 angles).
- Scale Amazon Sponsored Brands & Video Ads on high-converting non-brand terms.
- Deploy automated Klaviyo / WhatsApp abandoned checkout & post-purchase flows.
- Fix technical SEO index bloat and collection canonical tags.

### Month 3: Omnichannel Aggressive Scale
- Scale ad spend by 20% every 48 hours on proven winner creatives.
- Launch YouTube Video Action Campaigns (VAC) / Shorts to unlock net-new audiences.
- Optimize Meesho / Flipkart seller tiering and catalog visibility.
- Transition to ongoing Fractional CMO growth governance.

# GrowthYug — WhatsApp Outbound SOP
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-24

---

## CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Always lead with a specific observation** — never send a generic "we are a digital marketing agency" message
2. **One observation → one opportunity → one question** — keep it under 5 lines
3. **Never send bulk blasts** — every message must have at least one personalized element
4. **No follow-up within 48 hours** — give people time to respond
5. **Max 3 follow-ups per prospect** — then stop
6. **Always identify yourself and the company** — no fake personas

---

## RESEARCH PROCESS (Before Sending)

For every prospect, spend 5 minutes finding:
- Their website / store URL
- Their Amazon / Flipkart storefront (if applicable)
- Their current Meta/Google ad activity (check Meta Ad Library)
- One specific gap, opportunity or observation
- Their approximate monthly revenue tier (from product pricing × estimated volume)

**Do not send if you cannot find ONE specific observation.**

## PERSONALIZED AUDIT OUTREACH (High-Value Prospects)

For prospects with monthly revenue estimated above ₹25L / $30K, invest 30 minutes in a mini-audit before reaching out.

**Format:**

```
Hi [Name],

I spent 20 minutes auditing [Brand Name]'s ecommerce setup and found three things I'd address first:

1. [Specific issue 1 — e.g. "Your Meta ads are driving traffic to the homepage, not product pages — this alone is likely costing 30% of your conversion rate"]
2. [Specific issue 2 — e.g. "Your Amazon listing for [top product] is missing backend keywords for [category term]"]
3. [Specific issue 3 — e.g. "Your cart abandonment rate appears high — no visible WhatsApp or email recovery"]

Happy to walk you through what I'd fix and how — no commitment required.

Worth 20 minutes this week?

— [Your Name], GrowthYug
growthyug.in
```

## WHAT NEVER TO SEND

❌ "Hello sir, we are a digital marketing agency offering best services"  
❌ "We have 100+ clients and 8+ years experience" (unverified claims)  
❌ Any bulk template without a specific observation  
❌ Any message over 150 words  
❌ More than 3 follow-ups to the same person  
❌ Messages after 9pm or before 9am local time  
❌ Messages on Sunday unless prospect reached out first

## CRM TRACKING FIELDS

For every outbound prospect, log in CRM:
- Company name
- Contact name
- Phone
- LinkedIn / website
- Segment (D2C / Amazon / Shopify / Fashion / Beauty / Furniture / International)
- Revenue estimate
- Observation used in message
- Date sent
- Response (Yes / No / Not yet)
- Follow-up date
- Stage (Contacted / Responded / Call Booked / Proposal / Client / Lost)
- Loss reason if lost

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from ecommerce founders and operators before partnering with us.

How is GrowthYug different from a traditional digital marketing agency?

Traditional agencies are generalists who treat ecommerce marketing as disconnected services (one person running Meta ads, another doing SEO, nobody looking at inventory or Amazon). GrowthYug is an ecommerce-first growth partner. We connect customer acquisition, marketplace management, conversion rate optimization, and retention into one unified, measurable revenue engine.

What stages of ecommerce brands do you work with?

We primarily partner with growing D2C brands, marketplace sellers (Amazon/Flipkart), and established omnichannel brands looking to scale digital revenue from ₹5 Lakhs/month to over ₹1 Crore/month profitably. We also work with select venture-backed startups and established manufacturers launching direct-to-consumer divisions.

Do I need to hand over all my channels to GrowthYug at once?

No. While our full-funnel model delivers the highest compounding ROI, we often start by diagnosing and solving your single biggest bottleneck — whether that is high Amazon ACoS, unprofitable Meta ad spend, low Shopify conversion rates, or marketplace suspension risks — before expanding across channels.

How do you measure and report performance?

We do not report on vanity metrics like impressions or clicks. We measure true commercial outcomes: Blended ROAS, Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER), Amazon TACoS / ACoS, Net Contribution Margin, Store Conversion Rate, and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV). You receive real-time dashboard visibility and weekly strategic reviews.

How quickly can we get started?

Once you book a growth call, we conduct a comprehensive preliminary audit of your store, ad accounts, and marketplace listings within 48 to 72 hours. From there, we present an actionable growth roadmap and can initiate execution within 5 to 7 business days.

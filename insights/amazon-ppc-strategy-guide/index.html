Amazon PPC Strategy:
Complete Guide to Scaling Sales & Lowering TACoS

1. ACoS vs. TACoS: The True Metric of Amazon Brand Health

Sellers often make the mistake of looking at Advertising Cost of Sales (ACoS) in isolation. If you reduce bids to artificially lower ACoS from 30% to 15%, you might cut your sales volume in half, which degrades your organic keyword ranking and crashes overall brand revenue.

The TACoS Formula:
TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) = Total Ad Spend / Total Amazon Revenue (Organic + PPC)

A healthy Amazon brand experiences an expanding total revenue curve while TACoS declines into the 8% to 14% range. Calculate your brand's metrics on our interactive ROAS and ACoS calculator and read our ACoS reduction playbook.

2. The 4-Tier Amazon Campaign Architecture

To prevent bid cannibalization and ensure every rupee is deployed with intent, we structure campaigns into four distinct tiers:

Tier Campaign Type Match Types / Targets Strategic Objective
Tier 1: Auto Discovery Sponsored Products Auto Close-match, Loose-match, Substitutes, Complements Continuous algorithmic discovery of new customer search terms.
Tier 2: Broad / Phrase Research Sponsored Products Manual Broad & Phrase Match Keyword Targets Mid-funnel research on high-potential search queries with modest bids.
Tier 3: Exact Match Scaling Sponsored Products Manual Exact Match Single-Keyword Ad Groups (SKAGs) Aggressive top-of-search placement on validated high-converting root keywords.
Tier 4: Product ASIN Targeting (PAT) Sponsored Products PAT Competitor ASINs & Cross-Selling Own Brand Catalog Stealing sales from overpriced or poorly reviewed competitor listings.

3. Search Term Harvesting & Negative Keyword Isolation

The core engine of Amazon PPC optimization is weekly Search Term Harvesting:

  1. Download the 30-day Amazon Sponsored Products Search Term Report.
  2. Filter for search terms with ≥ 3 orders and an ACoS below target break-even.
  3. Promote these winning search terms into Tier 3 Exact Match campaigns with increased bid allocations.
  4. Immediately add the term as a Negative Exact Match in Tier 1 (Auto) and Tier 2 (Broad) campaigns to prevent internal bidding competition.
  5. Identify search terms with > 10 clicks and 0 orders and add them as Negative Exact to immediately eliminate wasted spend.

4. Sponsored Brands Video (SBV) & Sponsored Display (SD)

Sponsored Products alone are no longer enough to dominate Amazon SERPs. High-growth brands must deploy:

  • Sponsored Brands Video (SBV): Autoplaying mobile video ads that occupy 60% of the mobile viewport. Converting at 2x to 3x higher CTR than static image ads.
  • Sponsored Display Retargeting (Views Remarketing): Retargeting shoppers who visited your listing or competitor listings in the past 30 days but did not convert.

5. The PPC-to-Organic Ranking Flywheel

PPC sales on Amazon carry significant weight in the organic search ranking algorithm. When you concentrate exact match PPC spend on your top 5 high-volume category keywords, your sales velocity on those terms signals to Amazon that your product is highly relevant, boosting your organic rank from page 3 to page 1.

To maximize this effect, ensure your listing is fully optimized through our Amazon SEO and listing optimization services before turning up ad spend.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between ACoS and TACoS on Amazon?

ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) is Ad Spend divided by Ad Sales. TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) is Ad Spend divided by Total Amazon Sales (Ad Sales + Organic Sales). TACoS measures overall business profitability and indicates whether PPC is boosting organic rankings.

What is a healthy target TACoS for Amazon sellers?

For a mature product on Amazon, a healthy target TACoS is typically between 8% and 15%. For new product launches, a TACoS of 20% to 30% is acceptable during the initial 60 days to build sales velocity and review momentum.

Shopify Technical SEO

Shopify SEO: Complete Guide to
Growing Organic Traffic & Eliminating Duplicate Content

The tactical engineering guide to fixing Shopify's default duplicate URL structure, speeding up Liquid templates, structuring collection schema, and ranking collection pages.

Ajay Harchandani
Founder & CEO, GrowthYug • Published September 24, 2026 • 9 min read

Shopify powers millions of high-growth ecommerce brands, but its out-of-the-box SEO architecture contains critical flaws: rigid URL structures, bloated third-party apps injecting uncompressed JavaScript, and duplicate internal product links. Here is how our dedicated Shopify SEO agency team fixes these issues.

1. Fixing the Native Shopify Duplicate URL Flaw

By default, when users browse a collection grid, Shopify themes link to products using collection-nested paths:

https://yourstore.com/collections/summer-collection/products/linen-shirt

Even though Shopify adds a rel="canonical" pointing to https://yourstore.com/products/linen-shirt, having thousands of internal collection links pointing to the non-canonical version sends conflicting signals to Googlebot, wasting crawl budget and diluting PageRank.

The 1-Line Liquid Theme Fix

In your Shopify code editor, locate snippets/product-card.liquid or snippets/card-product.liquid. Find the URL filter and change:

<!-- BAD (Default Shopify) -->
<a href="{{ product.url | within: collection }}">

Change it to:

<!-- FIXED (Direct Canonical Link) -->
<a href="{{ product.url }}">

This simple edit ensures 100% of internal links on your store point directly to the canonical product URL.

2. Optimizing Shopify Collection Pages for High-Volume Keywords

Collection pages represent your biggest organic revenue opportunity. Maximize their ranking potential by:

  • Custom H1 & Intro Copy: Add an introductory paragraph (50–80 words) above the product grid defining the category and top benefits.
  • Extended SEO Content Below the Grid: Use Liquid to display a 300–500 word buying guide and FAQ accordion section at the bottom of the page, below pagination.
  • Automated Smart Collections: Build automated collections based on tags (e.g. SPF 50, Non-Comedogenic) to target long-tail search intent.

3. Core Web Vitals: App Bloat & Liquid Theme Optimization

Every time you install a Shopify app for popups, review widgets, or countdown timers, it injects tracking scripts into theme.liquid. Even after uninstalling, orphaned JavaScript files often remain.

Our website and CRO team audits and accelerates Shopify stores by:

  1. Removing orphaned script tags and old tracking pixels from uninstalled apps.
  2. Replacing heavy third-party app widgets with native, lightweight JavaScript and CSS.
  3. Enforcing responsive WebP/AVIF image rendering with dynamic srcset attributes.

4. Clean Shopify JSON-LD Structured Data Implementation

Many third-party review apps inject incomplete or conflicting Schema.org markup. Ensure your theme outputs a single unified JSON-LD script containing Product, AggregateRating, and Offer with dynamic currency and stock status.

5. Transforming the Shopify Blog into a Revenue Driver

Most Shopify blogs are ghost towns of thin 300-word updates. To drive real organic revenue, structure your blog posts around problem-based searches and embed dynamic product buy-buttons directly inside the content. Review our Shopify CRO playbook for PDP optimization.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Shopify natively create duplicate product URLs?

By default, Shopify themes link to products from collection pages using the path /collections/collection-name/products/product-name instead of the canonical /products/product-name. Although a canonical tag exists, internal links pointing to the collection-nested URL dilute internal PageRank and cause crawl waste.

How do you fix Shopify duplicate collection product links in Liquid?

In your Shopify theme's product card snippet, replace 'product.url | within: collection' with simply 'product.url'. This ensures all internal product links point directly to the canonical product URL.
